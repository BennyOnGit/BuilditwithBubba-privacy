Privacy Policy for Build it with Bubba iOS application.

BuildItWithBubba Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 2026

What we collect. When you create an account we collect your email address. When you upload an instruction manual, we store the file and the AI-generated guide created from it. When you talk with your AI guide, your speech is processed by our voice provider (ElevenLabs) and AI provider (Anthropic) to generate responses; conversation transcripts are stored with your account so your guide can remember where you left off. Purchases are processed by Apple and managed via RevenueCat; we never see or store payment details.

How we use it. Solely to provide the app's features: generating guides, powering voice conversations, tracking your build progress, and managing your purchases. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party processors. Supabase (data storage & authentication), Anthropic (AI processing), ElevenLabs (voice processing), RevenueCat (purchase management), Apple (payments).

Your rights. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in My Account → Profile → Delete Account. Questions or requests: [your email].

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.
